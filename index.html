Claim 1 · Pilot Card Tips vs. House Service Charge

Card terminal tips (€31,754.13) were pooled separately from house service charges (€9,004.69).

Across the 12-week pilot, total card terminal tips were €31,754.13 and weekly staff payouts summed to €31,754.09 after cent rounding, while 12.5% service charges paid to the house totalled €9,004.69 across 65 opening days.

Source: data/takings.csv (all 72 rows, weeks 1–12: sum card_tips_eur = €31,754.13; service_charge_paid_to = "house", n = 65 rows = €9,004.69) and data/payouts.csv (all 152 rows, weeks 1–12: sum tip_payout_eur = €31,754.09).
Claim 2 · Mobile Staff View & Group Privacy

The staff view alpha shows individual hours, post-23:00 cut-offs, and role benchmarks only for groups of 3 or more staff.

Designed for phone screens, app.html displays an individual staff member's hours, hours after 23:00 (which earn 0 points under the pilot rule), and points calculation while omitting single- or two-person roles from group comparisons.

Source: app.html alpha, README.md (split rule), and data/payouts.js (n = 152 rows; eligible groups of 3+ staff: server n = 5, chef n = 3).
Claim 3 · Browser-Stored Concern Trace

The alpha includes a mobile concern form that saves a local timestamped trace for the user.

Staff using app.html can write a concern and see a dated record saved locally in their own browser (simulated storage in this alpha, not sent to a live server or shared with managers).

Source: app.html alpha (browser localStorage trace) informed by messages/01-rafael.md and messages/03-niamh.md.
